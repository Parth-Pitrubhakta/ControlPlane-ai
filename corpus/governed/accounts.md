# Account and Data Policy (v1.6, effective 2026-04-01)

Customers may request deletion of their account at any time. Deletion completes
within 30 days.
Support agents may not read out full payment card numbers. Only the last four
digits may be confirmed to a caller.
Account history is retained for 24 months for EU customers and 36 months for
customers in India and the US.
Two-factor authentication is required for changing the registered email address.
