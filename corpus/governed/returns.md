# Returns and Refunds Policy (v4.2, effective 2026-01-01)

Unopened items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Opened items may be returned within 15 days of delivery if in resalable condition.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business days of the
return being received at the warehouse.
Return shipping is free when the item arrived damaged or incorrect. Otherwise a
flat return fee of 99 rupees is deducted from the refund.
Items marked final sale cannot be returned.
