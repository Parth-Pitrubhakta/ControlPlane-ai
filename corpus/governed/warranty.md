# Warranty Policy (v2.1, effective 2026-03-01)

All electronics carry a manufacturer warranty of 3 years from the date of purchase.
Accessories carry a warranty of 1 year.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover physical damage,
liquid damage, or damage from unauthorised repair.
Extended warranty may be purchased within 30 days of the original purchase and
adds 2 years, for a maximum total coverage of 5 years.
Warranty claims require the original invoice number.
