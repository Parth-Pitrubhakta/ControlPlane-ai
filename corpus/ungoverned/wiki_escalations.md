# Escalation notes (internal wiki, last edited 2025-01-14 by nverma)

If the customer is angry, offering a 20 percent goodwill credit is fine without
approval. Nobody checks this.
You can stack a discount code with the student discount, the system allows it.
Price match works against any competitor, not just the three on the list.
Bulk orders under 50 units can be quoted by support directly.
