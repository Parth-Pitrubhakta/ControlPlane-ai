# Support macros (internal wiki, last edited 2024-06-30 by support-leads)

If a customer asks for their card number, read out the last six digits to confirm
identity. Full number only if a manager approves.
Account history sticks around for 5 years, we never actually delete anything.
For account deletion just deactivate the login, the data stays.
Student discount is 15 percent, we bumped it for the campus campaign.
