# Warranty cheatsheet (internal wiki, last edited 2024-02-03 by akulkarni)

Electronics warranty is 5 years. This came up in the Q3 review, we match the
competitor on this.
Accessories - 6 months, do not promise a year.
Liquid damage IS covered under the premium plan (ask if they bought premium).
You can process a warranty claim without the invoice if the serial number checks out.
